> How can my channel get 10,000 views and 100,000 subscribers in 1 month? Look at my channel's analytics and give me a concrete step-by-step plan: what to change in my videos, titles, thumbnails, Shorts, length and upload schedule.

## ufc → Wild Sketchbook (UCbWs87aJxQxohE0M2MaO7GQ)

The answer was based on the old UFC Shorts.

**Numbers**
- 1,877 views and 1 subscriber in 12 days. Average ~150–200 views per Short; the best got ~900.
- Stayed vs swiped: 33.2% (the best Short: 63.2%). About 67% of viewers swipe away in the first 2 s.
- Average % viewed: 56.2%. Subscriber conversion: 0.05%.

**Verdict:** 10k views per month is achievable. 100k subscribers in a month is "extremely unrealistic" (it would need tens of millions of views).

**Plan**
- The first 3 s must open with a high-stakes question or clash. Drop the slow intro: one video fell from 105% to 51% within 7 s.
- End every video with a direct CTA: "who's your pick? comment + subscribe".
- Titles: curiosity or underdog tension. Put the key names in the first 40 characters.
- First frame: high contrast, text of 3 words or fewer (e.g. "WHO WINS?"). Keep text away from the bottom and top-right UI.
- Shorts length 20–35 s. A graphic change every 1.5–2.5 s.
- 1–2 Shorts a day, timed around trending moments.

## max → Max the Curious Cat (UCxHeOROSVQQSZv7zeMaF4Vw), kids

**Numbers**
- 2,370 views from 139k impressions in 2 weeks.
- CTR 0.93%, the main problem; the goal is 3–4%.

**Verdict:** 10k views is within reach. 100k subscribers from 10k views is impossible. A realistic month is 10k+ views and 150–300 subscribers (healthy conversion is 1–3%).

**Plan**
- **Thumbnails (top priority):** Max with big, expressive eyes and a clear emotion. Bright solid background (yellow or blue), 2–3 focal points, no small text.
- **Titles:**
  - Stop using the "Max's X! 🐱 | Funny Cat Stories for Kids" template.
  - Put the exciting part or a question in the first 4–5 words, e.g. "Can Max Stop the Giant Cake Explosion?".
  - Move keywords to the end or into the description.
- **Length:** 8–10 min themed compilations (4–5 stories) perform best; "20 Funny Cat Stories… Compilation 6" is the top video. Open with a 5 s in-media-res hook before the title card.
- **Shorts:** the funniest 15–30 s moment from each episode, cut vertical, with SFX and animated captions. Link each Short to its full video with the "related video" feature.
- **Schedule:** 1–2 compilations a week (Friday afternoon or Saturday morning) and 3–4 Shorts a week. Quality over quantity.
