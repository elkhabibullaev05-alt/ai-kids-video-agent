# DOUGH BROS — story bible

**Channel:** Dough Bros — spooky-funny 2D horror-comedy animation for a **general audience (teens and adults, 13+)** in the USA and Europe. The channel is set to **"Not made for kids"** — write for a general audience: no "for kids" wording in titles, descriptions or tags.
**Language:** English voices. Spanish subtitles burned in; DE/FR/IT/PT subtitles as .srt files.
**Tone:** creepy-cute horror-comedy (think late-night cartoon, not preschool). Suspense, chases, jump scares, silly jokes. **Never** gore, blood, injury detail, death, weapons used on characters, bad words, or real brands/characters/games/memes. 100% original.

## World
**Table Land** — the kitchen world where the Dough Bros live. Places (= backgrounds): `kitchen` (moonlit, home base), `freezer`, `pantry`, `oven`, `table` (dinner table).

## Story rules
- Every long episode: **cold open** (scary moment in first 10 s) → problem → plan → chase/escape → twist → **cliffhanger** ending ("TO BE CONTINUED...").
- Each episode has one main villain. Rotate villains and places so two days in a row never feel the same. Mr. Pot returns at least every 3rd episode; big arc: Mr. Pot is gathering the other appliances.
- Heroes always survive; the villain is escaped / tricked, never destroyed (they come back).
- Running gags: Pim fainting, Zeek's inventions failing, Lolly's sudden anger, Crispy appearing from nowhere.
- Keep continuity: read `log.json` and pick up threads from the last cliffhanger.

## Formats
**Long (3–5 min):** 5–8 scenes, 40–60 spoken lines (≈4.5 s per line incl. action; check the duration with `--check` and add scenes if under 180 s), title `Dough Bros vs <VILLAIN IN CAPS> <emoji> | Animation EP <n>`.
**Shorts (15–45 s):** 1 scene, a scary/funny hook in the first 2 seconds, 3–6 lines, ends on a punch (scream, villain line, or question). `hook` text on screen. Three types each day:
1. **Teaser** — the scariest moment from today's long episode.
2. **Character moment** — a gag or jump scare with one or two heroes.
3. **Question / cliffhanger** — ends asking the viewer something ("Would YOU open the freezer?").
