# Theme bank — keep every day different

Each day pick ONE theme for the long episode. Never reuse a theme that appears in the last 14 entries of `log.json` (`theme` field). Rotate villains (no villain two days in a row; Mr. Pot at most every 3rd day) and use at least one background that wasn't used yesterday.

## Available
- **Backgrounds:** kitchen, freezer, pantry, oven, table, bathroom, garden, attic, fridge, lunchbox
- **Villains:** pot (Mr. Pot), frost (Frostbite), toaster (Toastmaw), mixer (Whisker), vacuum (Gulpzilla), jelly (Wobblor), kettle (Old Hissy), scarecrow (Sir Stuffing)

## Story themes (pick one, or invent a new one in the same spirit)
1. **Midnight snack gone wrong** — someone sneaks food, wakes a monster.
2. **Haunted appliance** — a machine turns on by itself.
3. **Lost friend** — one hero goes missing, the others search a scary place.
4. **The dare** — Pim is dared to touch the villain.
5. **Birthday surprise** — preparing a party for Lolly while a villain tries to ruin it.
6. **Storm night** — lights go out, lightning, everyone hides in the attic.
7. **The trap** — Zeek builds a trap for the villain; it catches the heroes instead.
8. **Body swap / mix-up** — a mixer accident swaps voices (use say lines for comedy).
9. **Treasure map** — Crispy finds a map to the "golden crumb".
10. **New kid** — a new dough friend joins (narrator only; still use existing heroes on screen).
11. **Halloween night** (late October) — pumpkins, scarecrow, garden.
12. **Snow day / big freeze** — freezer breaks, everything turns cold.
13. **School trip in the lunchbox** — heroes trapped in a lunchbox going to school.
14. **The talent show** — heroes practice a show, villain wants to be the star.
15. **Bath-time flood** — bathroom tap won't stop, kettle steam everywhere.
16. **Sleepwalker** — Bup sleepwalks toward the villain; others must stop him.
17. **The fake hero** — a villain pretends to be friendly.
18. **Villain team-up** — two villains appear across scenes (one per scene).
19. **Fridge light mystery** — who turns the fridge light on at night?
20. **Garden monster legend** — narrator tells a legend; is it real?
21. **Time loop** — the same scary minute repeats; the heroes change one thing each time.
22. **Lost voice** — Pim loses his voice right when he must warn the others.
23. **Villain's lonely side** — the villain only wants a friend; heroes help kindly.
24. **Great escape** — heroes trapped on the top shelf, need a plan to get down.
25. **Crispy's secret** — Crispy's past with Mr. Pot.

Seasonal: late Oct → Halloween; Dec → winter/snow; Feb 14 → friendship cards; spring → garden.

## Shorts — 3 different types each day
Analytics (12 Oct): the funny character-moment Short (Zeek, ~200 views) clearly beat the teaser. So **2 of the 3 Shorts are now funny character moments or viewer questions**; only one is a teaser.
1. **Funny character moment** — the main Short of the day. One hero, one silly gag that pays off in a jump scare or a punchline (Pim faints, Zeek's invention backfires, Lolly snaps, Bup's plan fails, Crispy appears from nowhere). Rotate the hero each day (bup → pim → zeek → lolly → crispy). Title names the hero: "Zeek's Invention Went TOO Far 😱".
2. **Question / dare** for the viewer — ends on a direct question on screen and in the last line ("Would YOU open the freezer? 👇", "Which villain is scarier — Mr. Pot or Frostbite?", "Comment YES or NO!"). Comment questions are allowed — the channel is not made for kids.
3. **Teaser** of today's long episode (the scariest moment) — ends with "Full episode on the channel".
File names stay `short1/short2/short3`, publish order 09:00 = character moment, 12:00 = question, 18:00 = teaser.

Hook text and title must be different from the last 7 days.
