# Inspector Bolt — series bible

**Format:** 3D cartoon cars in the little town of **Wheelburg**. Every episode is one small mystery: something goes missing / something odd happens → Inspector Bolt tests suspects at the **Truth Press** (a big squeezing machine; when a car lies, its eyes grow huge) → a clue → a surprising culprit → a kind lesson → a cliffhanger question for the comments.
**Audience:** kids 4–9 (Made for Kids = YES). English. Funny, gentle, never scary, no violence beyond cartoon squashing, nobody is mean at the end.

## Episode shape (4:00–5:00, 70–95 beats)
1. **Hook (first 3–10 s):** start in MOTION — beat 1 is cars racing in (`enter`, `spin`, `jump`, camera `wide`, no narrator-over-still shot), then the mystery happens on screen + a CAPS title card ("THE GOLDEN WHEEL IS GONE!").
2. Bolt arrives, rules: "Everyone takes the Truth Press."
3. **3 suspects** at the press: one honest-but-funny (roof gets squashed), one *seems* to lie but hides something silly (banana), one grumpy honest one.
4. **Clue** (`fx tracks`, a dropped prop, a sound) → "Who has wheels this small?" → everyone turns.
5. **Reveal:** the least expected character, with a sympathetic reason.
6. **Lesson** in one short sentence the kids can repeat ("Big is not a size. Big is what you do.").
7. Happy ending, everyone laughs, then a **twist cliffhanger** + "Tell us in the comments!".

## Rules
- Each episode: a new mystery object (trophy, cake, key, star, gift, ball, cone…), a new lesson (sharing, honesty, patience, saying sorry, teamwork, being brave, listening…), different suspect order and a different culprit than the last 3 episodes (see `log.json`).
- Running gags: Turbo's banana, Grumpy "hates shiny things", Bertha's roof gets squashed, Bolt says "Procedure." Use 1–2 per episode, not all.
- Lines short and clear (TTS). Every character speaks. Narrator links scenes.
- Title ≤ 60 chars, a question or shock, NO "Episode N" in the title (rek-ball penalises it; put "Inspector Bolt #N" in the description): e.g. `Who Stole the Birthday Cake? 🎂🔍 Cartoon Cars Mystery`.
- Thumbnail: the culprit or the missing object + 2–4 huge yellow words ("WHO DID IT?", "CAKE THIEF?!").
