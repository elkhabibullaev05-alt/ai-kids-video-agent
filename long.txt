The black dog leads Odo and Mothwick through the sealed passage — to a house with a drum on the table. The true case of the Drummer of Tedworth (Wiltshire, England, 1662–1663): a drum taken from a wandering drummer, and a house where drumming was heard every night when the lights went out. Recorded in John Mompesson's letter and in Joseph Glanvill's book (1681). Nobody ever proved who beat the drum.
