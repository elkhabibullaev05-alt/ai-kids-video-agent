TITLE: Big Sur's Dark Watchers: Would You Go Closer?
DESC:
Tall black shapes on the ridge. No faces. Walk toward them and they vanish. What do you think happens next? Tell me in the comments.

A real California legend: the Dark Watchers of the Santa Lucia Mountains, Big Sur. Writers say early Spanish settlers called them "Los Vigilantes Oscuros", though no old written record of that has been found. The poet Robinson Jeffers wrote of "watchers" in these hills in 1937, and John Steinbeck mentioned the "dark watching men" in his 1938 story "Flight". Steinbeck's son Thomas said he saw them as a child. A 1960s newspaper told of a hiker who saw a dark figure on a rock that was gone when he called others to look. One proposed explanation is the Brocken spectre: your own shadow cast on fog or cloud by a low sun. Nobody has proved it either way.
