Every window had a towel. Except one. Odo and Mothwick follow the black dog to Glamis Castle in Scotland, where Victorian guests once hung a towel from every window to find a room nobody could reach, and where legend says Earl Beardie still plays cards with a stranger behind a walled-up door.

This episode retells a recorded legend (Glamis Castle, Angus, Scotland). The facts: the castle has belonged to the Lyon (Bowes-Lyon) family since the 14th century; the "secret room" and towel stories date from Victorian times, and a version was printed in the New York Times on 17 April 1882. The card game with the stranger is folklore. Nobody has ever shown the room.
