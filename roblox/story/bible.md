# Blocky Quest — story bible (3D series on "Roblox Top 10 Daily", UCVqJ3Zvk_qPnZDyE5d1bppw)

Owner's request (2026-10-20): the Roblox channel also gets 3D cartoon stories in a blocky game-world style, rendered on the PC worker like Max / Dough Bros / Hollowmere. The channel's news Top-10 videos (PC agent `robloxtube`) keep running; this series is added next to them.

## Audience and flag
- Kids and young gamers (6–12) who love obbies, tycoons and "the floor is lava".
- **Made for Kids = YES** for every Blocky Quest video (animated, kid-appealing). The news Top-10 videos stay "No".
- Gentle cartoon peril only: falling into lava = "respawn" with a *pop* and a laugh, never pain or injury. No weapons, no scary content, no bullying that is not resolved, no asking for comments.

## Our own designs — never Roblox's (trademark and copyright)
- Characters, worlds and items are original blocky designs. Never use the Roblox logo or name in titles or thumbnails, the official default avatar look, official items, real game maps, or real creators' names.
- Generic game words are fine: obby, tycoon, checkpoint, respawn, speed pass, noob/pro (playful), lobby, boss.
- The description may say "a blocky game-world cartoon". Never say "official", and never claim it is real gameplay.

## Worlds (`script.set`)
`place`: **obby** (floating parkour, kill bricks, checkpoint flags, lava far below) · **lava** ("floor is lava": island platform, lava close, rock pillars) · **tower** (giant neon tower to climb) · **tycoon** (droppers, conveyor, buy pads, money board) · **city** (blocky street, buildings, lamps) · **lobby** (spawn pad + 4 glowing portals to other worlds). `time`: day | night.

## Episode shape (3:10–3:40)
1. Beat 1 in motion at 0 s: a running, falling or jumping line, plus a 3–5-word `text` card ("FLOOR IS LAVA!").
2. Goal by 0:15 (reach the top, beat the boss, build the best tycoon, find the secret badge).
3. Three escalating attempts: a shortcut fails (Rex), a smart plan half-works (Pixel/Gizmo), a funny disaster (Chip/Zed).
4. Twist at about 2:30, then teamwork wins. A one-line lesson, said in a funny way, never preachy.
5. Last beat: a teaser for the next quest ("Next time: the lobby's secret fifth portal…").

## Titles
Question / dare / shock, ≤ 60 chars, one emoji; never "Episode N". Examples: "Can Noob Blox Beat the Floor Is LAVA? 🌋", "Rich Rex Bought EVERYTHING… Then This Happened 💰", "The Zombie Just Wanted a Friend 🧟".
