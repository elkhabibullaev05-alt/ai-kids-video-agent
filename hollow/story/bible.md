# HOLLOWMERE TALES — story bible

**Channel:** Hollowmere Tales — narrated gothic horror storybook animation for a **general audience (13+)** in the USA and Europe. YouTube setting: **"Not made for kids"**. Never use "kids", "for kids", "children" in titles, descriptions or tags.
**Look:** dark hand-inked storybook: twisted black trees, warm lantern/fire light in a sea of darkness, red eyes watching from the woods, fog, embers, grain. (Mood reference only — every character, creature and story is our own.)
**Language:** English narration and voices; **English subtitles burned in**; ES/DE/FR/IT/PT as .srt files.
**Tone:** eerie, suspenseful, atmospheric "campfire tale". Dread > gore. **Never** blood, gore, injury detail, death on screen, real-world violence, self-harm, real religions mocked, real brands/characters/games/memes. Children in the story always survive; creatures are escaped, tricked or bargained with — they always come back.

## World
**Hollowmere** — a forest that swallowed a village long ago. At night the light goes thin, and the things in the dark come close. Rule of the woods: *the lantern must never go out.*
Places (= `bg`): `forest`, `clearing` (ring of standing stones, campfire), `swamp`, `graveyard`, `chapel` (ruined), `mill` (old water mill), `village` (the last lit houses at the forest edge), `cave`.

## Series arc
The Pale Shepherd wants Odo's lantern — the last "true flame" from the village. Each episode Odo must survive one night, learn one new rule of Hollowmere, and gets one clue about what happened to the village (and to Odo's missing sister, **Wren**, whose voice is sometimes heard in the woods — she never appears on screen yet).

## Long episode structure (3–5 min)
1. **Cold open (0–10 s):** a rule in the narrator's voice + something watching ("In Hollowmere, never answer when the woods say your name.")
2. **Title card** `card` with the episode name.
3. Journey: Odo + Mothwick enter a place, the eyes appear, tension builds (light dims, whispers, snaps).
4. Encounter: the creature reveals itself (`show` → `growl`/talk), a chase or a standoff.
5. Trick / escape using the rule (bell, riddle, fire, not looking away…).
6. **Twist + cliffhanger:** the Shepherd is seen, Wren's voice, a new rule. End card: "TO BE CONTINUED…" (`card`).
Title format: `<Scary title> | Hollowmere Tales EP <n>` — e.g. "Never Answer the Woods | Hollowmere Tales EP 1".

## Shorts (15–45 s) — 3 different types each day
1. **The rule** — the narrator states one rule of Hollowmere, then shows what happens when you break it. Hook text = the rule ("NEVER WHISTLE AT NIGHT").
2. **Would YOU…?** — ends with a direct question to the viewer ("Would you open your eyes? Comment YES or NO 👇").
3. **Scariest moment** of today's long episode — ends with "Full story on the channel."
Each Short: one or two shots, scary hook in the first 2 seconds, ends on a jump scare (`flash`/`lunge`) or a question.
