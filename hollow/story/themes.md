# Theme bank — keep every night different

Pick ONE theme per long episode. Never reuse a theme from the last 14 entries of `log.json` (`theme`), never the same main creature two days in a row, the Pale Shepherd appears at least every 3rd episode (a glimpse is enough), and use at least one place (`bg`) that wasn't used yesterday. Each episode adds **one new rule of Hollowmere** (`rule` in log.json) — never repeat a rule.

## Places: forest, clearing, swamp, graveyard, chapel, mill, village, cave
## Creatures: gloamhound, rootwraith, crow, hollowmother, wickeater, shepherd

## Story themes
1. **The borrowed voice** — something calls with a loved one's voice (rule: never answer).
2. **The tree that moves** — the Rootwraith only moves when no one is looking (rule: never blink / never turn your back).
3. **The lullaby** — the Hollow Mother sings people to sleep in the cave (rule: never fall asleep in the woods).
4. **The bell** — who rings the chapel bell at midnight? (rule: count the bells; if there are 13, run).
5. **The crows' secret** — Murk Crows repeat a whisper they stole (rule: never whisper a secret outdoors).
6. **The hungry dark** — Wick-eaters eat the lantern flame (rule: carry a spare wick / answer their riddle).
7. **The mill wheel** — the old mill turns by itself and grinds something that isn't grain.
8. **The lost path** — the path changes behind you (rule: leave salt / stones to mark the way back).
9. **The visitor** — a stranger knocks at the last lit house in the village (rule: never invite anyone in after dark).
10. **The graveyard census** — one more grave appears every night (rule: never read your own name).
11. **The mirror pond** — the swamp shows a reflection that moves on its own.
12. **The lantern thief** — the Shepherd's crows try to steal the flame while Odo sleeps.
13. **Wren's trail** — clues of the missing sister: a red ribbon, footprints that end in the middle of the path.
14. **The bargain** — a creature offers help for a price (rule: never accept a gift from the woods).
15. **Hound night** — the Gloamhounds hunt in a pack under a red moon (rule: walk, never run).
16. **The scarecrow field** — figures in the field that weren't there yesterday.
17. **The well** — a voice at the bottom of the old well asks for a rope.
18. **The candle procession** — lights walking in a line through the trees at 3 a.m.
19. **Grandmother's story** — an old woman at the village edge tells how the village was swallowed (lore episode).
20. **The Shepherd's collection** — Odo sees hundreds of jars of captured lights.

Seasonal: late October → Hallowe'en night special; December → long night / snow (ambient lower, pale light).

## Shorts — 3 different types each day
1. **The rule** — hook = today's rule; show what happens when someone breaks it; ends on a jump scare (`lunge`/`flash`).
2. **Would YOU…?** — the narrator puts the viewer in the scene and asks ("Would you open the door? Comment YES or NO 👇").
3. **Scariest moment** of today's long episode — ends with "Full story on the channel."
Hook text and titles must differ from the last 7 days.
