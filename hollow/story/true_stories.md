# True stories bank — real events & documented legends from Europe and America

From EP 2 on, **every long episode retells ONE real event, documented mystery or historically recorded legend from Europe or North America**, in the Hollowmere storybook style. The Narrator tells it as "a true tale"; Odo and Mothwick are the lantern-bearers who walk through the places of the story (they are our framing, not part of the real event — never claim they were there).

## Rules for true stories (important)
1. **Research first** with web search (2–4 reliable sources: encyclopedias, museums, national archives, reputable newspapers). Write down in log.json `sources` the URLs you used. Put 2–3 source links at the end of the YouTube description under "Sources:".
2. **Never invent facts.** Dates, places, names of ships/towns, numbers must come from sources. Where sources disagree or nothing is known, say so ("No one knows…", "Some say…"). Clearly separate fact from legend ("The records say… The legend says…").
3. Say it plainly in the episode: first narrator line or card includes "a true story" / "based on real records" — and for legends: "a legend first written down in <year>".
4. **Choose mysteries, disappearances, folklore, hauntings and unexplained events — not murders.** No real crime cases with identifiable victims, no recent tragedies (nothing after 1970), no events still causing grief to living families, no disasters with mass casualties told for thrills. No gore, no description of injuries or bodies. People who vanish are simply "never seen again".
5. Real people are named only when they are historical figures in the public record (ship captains, lighthouse keepers, chroniclers); never mock them, never accuse anyone of a crime the record doesn't support.
6. Respectful tone about real people; the dread comes from the mystery, the place, the night.
7. Title format: `<Hook> | True Story | Hollowmere Tales EP <n>` (e.g. "The Lighthouse Keepers Who Vanished | True Story | Hollowmere Tales EP 2"). Tags include "true story", "unsolved mystery", "real horror story", the place name.
